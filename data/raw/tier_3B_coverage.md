## Cross-cutting notes
- posted_date_source: Accenture relative_text; Arup none; all others portal (Capgemini = index date, approximate; IBM dcdate and Workday startDate can be re-stamps).
- Carry-over rule: where the same (firm, req_id) appears in data/raw_prev_2026-10-10, -10-15 or -10-21 with an earlier date, the earliest date is used and rows first seen before 10-19 are dropped.
- Years rule: lower bound >= 6 dropped; ranges with lower bound <= 5 kept. Accenture Consultant tier kept only when the JD body states <= 3 years minimum.
- Band mapping: Team Lead/Consultant (Accenture) = Consultant; Experienced Senior = Senior Associate; Junior Consultant = Associate Consultant; Senior X Consultant = Senior Consultant.
- Sponsorship flag from JD text only, conservative (clearance and right-to-work phrases count as stated-not-offered); null where nothing stated.
- Summary, qualifications and keywords are heuristic extractions; jd_text is the full portal text.
