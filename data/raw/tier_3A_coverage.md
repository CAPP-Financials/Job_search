# Tier 3A coverage (run 2026-10-10, window posted_date >= 2026-10-03)

Totals: 73 roles kept across 9 of 15 firms (Sia 29, Wavestone 19, Kearney 10, PA 5, Oliver Wyman 3, OC&C 3, Simon-Kucher 2, Roland Berger 1, CRA 1). Output: data/raw/tier_3A.jsonl (validated: required keys, allowed enums, dates, 3-line summaries, non-empty required quals, no replacement chars, full country names).
Discovery used only each firm's own portal or the ATS behind it (portal JSON/Atom/RSS APIs, plus Playwright rendering). No LinkedIn or job boards. Firecrawl used 4 times, all for ADL (see blocker). Nothing was applied to, signed into, or submitted.

Common exclusion rules applied across all firms: Manager/Principal/Director/Partner+, interns and campus/new-grad intakes (judged from the JD text: dropped when the only experience asked is internships or a first job, or it is a graduate cohort; kept when a stated range overlaps 2-5 years), more than 5 years minimum experience (min 5 is kept: Kearney 5-7 and 5-10, PA 5+; Kearney 6-8 dropped), back-office roles, and technical roles with no honest A or B fit (cybersecurity/OT security, SAP configuration, data/ML engineers and architects, IT audit, legal). Those no-fit roles are counted per firm so nothing disappears silently.

## Data quality notes
- posted_date_source: portal for OW, PA, RB, Sia, SK, CRA, Wavestone; relative_text for Kearney (estimated from board ages); none for OC&C.
- Years of experience are regex-extracted from JD text (residency/clearance sentences excluded) and may be null where the JD states none.
- sponsorship_flag: "likely-needed" is the default for non-India roles with no statement; "stated-not-offered" where a JD asks for existing work authorization or clearance (OW NY, Sia US, Simon-Kucher US); "n/a-local" for the only India role (OW Hyderabad).
- Salaries captured where posted: OW NY $190,000-$240,000; Kearney US SOP $123,000-$127,000 (Sr BA) and $188,000-$194,000 (Associate); PA Boston $115,800-$121,900; Simon-Kucher Boston/Chicago $125,000-$155,000; CRA DC $92,500-$105,000; Sia Philadelphia $118,300-$122,400.
- Track tags: only 6 roles are AB or B (Wavestone energy roles whose JD mentions energy transition/decarbonisation x4 AB, Sia Energy, Utilities & Low Carbon Consultant AB, Sia Senior ESG Consultant FS B). Energy titles whose JD has no decarbonisation/energy-transition language are tagged A for every firm. Everything else is A.
- country uses full English names; multi-country postings are joined with "; ".
- India coverage: the only India role in the window across tier 3A is OW Hyderabad Analyst/Senior Analyst (Private Capital). Kearney's Gurugram "Value Realization" roles (7/31) and Sia India roles are outside the window or back-office.
