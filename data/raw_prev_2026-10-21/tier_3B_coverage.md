## Cross-cutting notes
- posted_date_source values: Accenture relative_text (date = 2026-10-21 minus N days); Arup none; everything else portal (Capgemini = index date, most stamped 10-15 by a mass re-index, treat as approximate; IBM dcdate is a re-stamp date).
- sponsorship_flag set from JD text only; India roles tagged n/a-local; null where nothing stated. Many US public-sector roles show stated-not-offered (citizenship, clearance, work-authorisation language); the regex also catches 'eligible to work in' phrasing, so treat it as conservative.
- Band mapping: 'Senior Associate' kept as Senior Associate; BDO 'Experienced Senior' mapped to Senior Associate; 'Consultant/Senior Consultant' and '(Senior) Consultant' combined titles mapped to Senior Consultant; Junior Consultant mapped to Associate Consultant; Accenture band = careerLevel. Title and years_exp are the authority.
- The 6+ yrs rule was applied from years parsed out of the JD text (lower bound >= 6 dropped); years_exp is parsed by regex and may pick up a preferred rather than a required figure. Ranges such as 5-8 or 3-8 are left in.
- Summary, qualifications and keywords were extracted heuristically from the JD text; jd_text is the full portal text (Slalom, A&M, Arup bodies cleaned from HTML).
- Scope seen counts for Workday firms come from list locationsText (multi-location postings are undercounted). Window = posted_date >= 2026-10-14; Workday detail startDate used for the final date.
- Carry-over rule (applied to every firm): where the same (firm, req_id) appears in data/raw_prev_2026-10-10 or data/raw_prev_2026-10-15 with an earlier date, the earliest observed date is used and rows whose earliest date is before 10-14 are dropped, because portal re-stamps (Accenture updateDate, IBM dcdate, Gartner datePosted, Workday startDate) otherwise pull old postings into the window. 19 rows were removed this way or by the years review; they are listed per firm above.
- Years review: every jd_text was rescanned for any figure >= 6 years and reviewed by hand; years_of() returns the smallest lower bound found, so years_exp can under-report a headline ask, which is why the manual pass was needed. Ranges with a lower bound under 6 (for example Capgemini 5-8, A&M 3-8, Accenture 3-8) are kept.
- Not covered / blockers: Grant Thornton UAE detail pages (login) and SG/HK/MY/SA hosts, BDO India and other member-firm ATS, Protiviti Middle East, Arup posting dates, Slalom ids outside 3780-3945, Infosys Consulting own portal (infosysconsulting.com cert error, infosys.com 403), Carbon Trust and South Pole dates, Accenture in-en 10,000 row cap.
