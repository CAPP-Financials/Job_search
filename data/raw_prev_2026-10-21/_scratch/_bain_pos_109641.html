        Description & Requirements

        About Bain & Company

        Bain & Company is a global consultancy that helps the world's most ambitious change makers define the future. With 63 offices across 38 countries, we work alongside our clients as one team to achieve extraordinary results, outperform the competition, and redefine industries.

        We combine deep expertise with a vibrant ecosystem of digital innovators to deliver better, faster, and more enduring outcomes. Since our founding in 1973, we've measured our success by the success of our clients, maintaining one of the highest levels of client advocacy in the industry.

        The Role

        The Video Centre of Excellence, part of Bain's Brand Studio within the Marketing team, creates high-quality video and podcast content for audiences around the world.

        We're looking for a Video Content Creator who thinks social-first, has strong creative instincts, and knows what makes audiences stop scrolling. You'll create compelling video content from concept to delivery, working across a wide variety of formats while collaborating with producers, marketers and subject matter experts.

        This is an exciting opportunity to transform some of the world's most complex business ideas into engaging, visually compelling content that informs, entertains and inspires. You'll also be at the forefront of applying AI to creative workflows, experimenting with new tools and helping shape how we create content in the future.

        What You'll Do

        You'll create end-to-end video content across a range of formats, including:

        • Social-first video for LinkedIn, Instagram and YouTube
        • Thought leadership and executive content
        • Employee-generated content (EGC)
        • Event coverage
        • Explainer videos
        • Podcast and interview content

        Your responsibilities will include:

        Content Creation & Production

        • Develop creative concepts and visual stories that deliver against communication objectives while engaging audiences.
        • Plan, shoot and edit video content across multiple formats and styles.
        • Build strong narratives with compelling hooks, pacing and storytelling techniques.
        • Operate cameras, lighting, audio equipment and production rigs across a variety of filming environments.
        • Edit content using Adobe Premiere Pro, with opportunities to develop your motion graphics skills in After Effects.
        • Apply colour grading, sound design, graphics and visual effects to produce polished, high-quality content.
        • Ensure all work meets Bain's quality standards, brand guidelines and accessibility requirements before review.

        Creative Development

        • Stay ahead of emerging trends across social platforms and translate them into creative ideas that feel fresh and relevant.
        • Contribute to brainstorming sessions and creative treatments with a creator mindset.
        • Balance creativity with strategic thinking to ensure every piece of content achieves its purpose while reflecting Bain's brand.

        AI & Innovation

        • Use AI tools throughout the creative process, from ideation and scripting to production and post-production.
        • Continuously explore new technologies and SaaS tools that improve efficiency and creative quality.
        • Share learnings and best practices with the wider team to help evolve our approach to AI-powered content creation.

        Collaboration

        • Build strong relationships with producers, marketers and internal stakeholders.
        • Communicate clearly throughout projects, managing expectations and identifying potential challenges early.
        • Estimate production timelines accurately and contribute to efficient project planning.
        • Adapt quickly to changing priorities while maintaining high creative standards.

        About You

        You'll have 2-4 years' experience creating video content from concept through to final delivery, ideally with a strong emphasis on social-first content.

        You may have built your experience in:

        • An in-house creative or marketing team
        • A production or post-production company
        • A creative or brand agency
        • As a freelance creator with a diverse portfolio

        A degree in Film, Media, Communications or a related discipline is beneficial but not essential. We're far more interested in your portfolio, creativity and technical ability than your formal qualifications.

        What You'll Bring

        Creative Storytelling

        You know how to tell stories that connect with audiences. You're curious, imaginative and understand what makes content resonate beyond simply following trends.

        Production Skills

        You're confident operating professional camera, lighting and audio equipment, and can adapt your approach to different environments and production requirements.

        Editing Expertise

        You have strong editing skills in Adobe Premiere Pro and experience creating content across multiple formats. You're comfortable with colour grading, audio editing, motion graphics and visual effects, with growing experience in After Effects and AI-powered creative tools.

        Producing & Directing

        You can independently execute creative briefs, organise shoots and confidently work with contributors to capture engaging content.

        Attention to Detail

        You take pride in producing polished work, ensuring every edit, frame and audio mix meets a high standard while adhering to brand guidelines.

        Adaptability

        You're comfortable managing multiple projects, working to deadlines and adjusting quickly when priorities change.

        Collaboration

        You enjoy working with cross-functional teams, value feedback and communicate effectively throughout the creative process.

        Growth Mindset

        You're eager to learn, continuously improve your craft and embrace new technologies that push creative boundaries.

        Why Join Bain?

        At Bain, you'll have the opportunity to create work that reaches global audiences while collaborating with talented colleagues across marketing, consulting and creative disciplines.

        We're consistently recognised as one of the world's best places to work and are committed to building an inclusive culture where everyone can thrive. We believe diversity, collaboration and continuous learning are fundamental to delivering extraordinary work.

        If you're passionate about visual storytelling, excited by the possibilities of AI, and ready to create content that stands out in a crowded digital landscape, we'd love to hear from you.
