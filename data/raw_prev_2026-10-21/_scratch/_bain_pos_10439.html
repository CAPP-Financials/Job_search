        Description & Requirements

        WHO YOU’LL WORK WITH:

        Bain employees are investigative, creative, thoughtful managers and collaborators who work together as an integrated team, delivering extraordinary outcomes for our clients.  As a Senior Manager at Bain, you will oversee the formulation and implementation of strategic recommendations to improve the performance of Bain’s clients by providing thought leadership and daily oversight to your case team as well as collaborating closely with our clients.  Your analytical, interpersonal, creative thinking, process management, business acumen and leadership skills will be called upon from day one.  Senior Managers assume responsibility for value creation and delivery of key client recommendations, provide leadership, coaching and supervision to case team members, and build and strengthen client relationships

        WHAT YOU’LL DO: 

        • As a Senior Manager, you’ll quickly join and lead case teams serving our clients and gain exposure to a spectrum of assignments that are complex in scale and scope, intellectually demanding, and highly deadline driven.  You will be in the thick of action as you work side-by-side with some of the best in the business.  And you will partner with our clients to action and impact.  Every day is different, but a sample of responsibilities you can expect to accomplish include:
        • Be intimately involved in each client’s organization and industry, working on complex business problems 
        • Identify value creation opportunities in each client situation 
        • Drive analysis, design, and implementation of solutions, develop tailor-made solutions and work closely with clients 
        • Provide intellectual and thought leadership to the team to ensure the highest quality of analytical and conceptual output 
        • Gather and analyse information, formulate and test hypotheses, and develop recommendations for presentation to client management and work with clients to implement change to achieve desired outcomes 
        • Build and maintain consensus with clients on case goals, critical issues, workplan, implications, recommendations, and implementation plan.  Develop and secure client commitment to recommendations and implementation plans 
        • Cultivate talented groups of people and collaborate with multiple teams. You will manage and mentor teams 
        • Effectively manage the formal and informal professional development of case team members by supporting appropriate skills development plans, providing coaching and regular performance feedback
        • Organize the work of the team by allocating clear responsibilities among caseteam members while balancing the delivery of caseteam goals with the professional development and satisfaction of individual case team members
        • Contribute to the key firm building initiatives (Practice/Capability area knowledge building and IP generation, Training, Recruiting, Mentorship, office initiatives, etc)

        Integration Support:

        To ensure new Senior Managers are well equipped for a smooth start to a successful career, Bain offers best in class integration support to all Senior Managers embarking on a professional journey with Bain, spanning the first 12-18 months of your career at Bain.

        The integration plan will focus on three key pillars of success:

        • Strong support from an Integration Steering Committee of experienced Bainies acting collectively to support your growth and development at Bain. This includes active support to get you involved in client engagements, help initiate and expand your network at the firm, and clarify and address your questions and provide feedback.  Your integration will also be supported by a dedicated mentor, a senior member of the leadership team and local practice leadership, as well as a colleague friend
        • A comprehensive onboarding plan with a focus on building out your Bain capabilities for long term success. This can include playing a variety of roles on teams for short periods of time sometimes starting with a Consultant role or shadowing a current Senior Manager, if needed, to quickly learn the Bain craft and progress to the full skill set of a Bain Senior Manager
        • Bain learning including on the job experiences, peer-to-peer experience sharing, and formal global training programs

        ABOUT YOU:

        Senior Managers are instrumental to our clients’ and teams’ success, as well as to our success as a firm. Qualified candidates must meet a set of criteria we deem essential for those interested in the Senior Manager role:

        • Typically, a minimum of seven years of work experience including demonstrated career progression to a Senior Manager-equivalent level at another consulting firm
        • Rigorous analytical skills, ability to excel at solving problems that require sound logical reasoning, creative thinking, fluency with numbers and ability to untangle complex issues
        • Highly motivated, energetic, enthusiastic, committed and inclusive team player. Authentic in a case team setting
        • Ability to exercise business judgement, communicate persuasively and generate insights to build trusting client relationships
        • Ability to project poise, confidence and professional maturity in both internal and external situations; engaging and concise with good interpersonal “presence”, comfortable both in the C-suite and team room settings
        • Demonstrated outstanding team management skills while working on a complex assignment
        • Flexibility and ability to serve and address a wide range of client assignments; Ability to meet tight deadlines
        • Articulate and compelling verbal, written and presentation skills are vital
        • Entrepreneurial, proactive, pragmatic, someone who “rolls up the sleeves” and gets things done
        • Insightful and creative individual with an affinity for data-driven insights to decision making and passion for a track record of demonstrable results
        • Great colleague, teammate and mentor; passionate about the task at hand and fun to be around
        • Undergraduate/Masters/MBA/PhD from a top program with strong academic results
        • Please note that language requirements vary.  Fluency in English is required for all offices. Please review the office information for requirements for your office of interest.

        COMPENSATION:
        For all locations, the good-faith, reasonable annualized full-time compensation for this role is commensurate with competitive geographic market rates. In some locations, compensation may vary based on several factors including, but not limited to, relevant experience, education, licensure/certifications, training, and skill level.

        Bain's comprehensive benefits and wellness programs are designed to support your emotional, physical, and professional needs:

        • Wellbeing: Health plans for employees and families that go beyond the basics to include mental, emotional, and behavioral health resources, counseling, assistance, and digital wellness tools
        • Financial Security: Life, accident, disability, as well as tax efficient programs and retirement savings, where applicable, and extras through various expense and office policies
        • Flexibility: Paid holidays, vacation, personal sick time, flexibility, and accommodation. Plus, flexible breaks and family leave policies
        • Learning: A culture of mentorship across all roles, and full lifecycle career support, training, and planning

        Compensation for this role in the United States includes base salary, annual discretionary performance bonus, 401(k) plan with an annual employer contribution based on years of service, and Bain's best in class benefits package (details listed above).

        The annualized base compensation for first-year Senior Managers in the United States is $222,000. This role may also be eligible for other elements of discretionary compensation.

        Note for Italy

        In accordance with applicable Italian pay transparency requirements, Bain & Company provides information regarding the compensation applicable to this position.

        The annualized base salary for this role in Italy is €115,000 gross per year.

        This compensation has been determined based on objective and gender-neutral criteria and reflects the compensation normally applicable to this position at the time of publication.

        This role is eligible for an annual performance bonus of up to 98% of base salary.

        Bonus awards are discretionary and actual bonus outcomes depend on individual and firm performance.

        This position is subject to the provisions of Contratto Collettivo Nazionale di Lavoro per i Dirigenti Industria
