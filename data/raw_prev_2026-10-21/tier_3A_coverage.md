# Tier 3A coverage (run 2026-10-21, window posted_date >= 2026-10-14)

Totals: 48 roles kept across 9 of 15 firms (Sia 20, Wavestone 7, Kearney 7, PA 4, L.E.K. 4, OC&C 3, Oliver Wyman 1, Simon-Kucher 1, Dalberg 1). Output: data/raw/tier_3A.jsonl, data/raw/tier_3A_scopes.json. Validated: schema keys identical to the 10-15 file, band and track enums, dates >= 2026-10-14 or null with source none, 3-line summaries, non-empty required qualifications, jd_text >= 500 chars, no replacement characters, no duplicate URLs, no ask with minimum 6+ years.

Discovery used only each firm's own portal or the ATS behind it (public JSON/Atom/RSS APIs, plain HTML, Playwright renders for Kearney, Simon-Kucher and OC&C page 2). No LinkedIn or job boards. Firecrawl was not needed. Nothing was applied to, signed into, or submitted.

Common exclusion rules: Manager/Principal/Director/Partner+, interns, apprenticeships and campus/new-grad intakes (including 'Consultant Junior' CDI ads that ask for no experience), minimum 6+ years (min 5 kept), back-office roles, and technical roles with no honest A or B fit (cyber, SAP, data/ML/software engineers and architects, test automation).

## Scope check (global / UK / India, then sponsorship-friendly markets)

Every firm in this tier runs one global board or ATS that carries all countries, so UK and India are recorded as via-global with the number of postings found by location (zero is recorded as zero, not skipped). OC&C's portal is the UK-run site, so its UK scope is ok. Details with URLs, plus UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Singapore, Hong Kong, Malaysia, South Africa and Philippines counts, are in tier_3A_scopes.json.
