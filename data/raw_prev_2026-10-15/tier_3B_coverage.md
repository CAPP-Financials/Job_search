## Cross-cutting notes
- posted_date_source values: Accenture relative_text (date = 2026-10-15 minus N days; "within last 24 hours" = 10-15); Arup none; everything else portal (Capgemini = index date, most stamped 10-15 by a mass re-index, treat as approximate; IBM dcdate is a re-stamp date).
- sponsorship_flag set from JD text only; roles in India tagged n/a-local; null where nothing stated. Many Guidehouse/Protiviti/FTI/BRG US roles show stated-not-offered (citizenship, clearance or work-authorisation language); the regex also catches "eligible to work in" phrasing, so treat it as conservative.
- Band mapping: "Senior Associate" kept as Senior Associate; "(Senior) Associate" mapped to Associate; BDO "Experienced Senior" mapped to Senior Associate; "Consultant/Senior Consultant" combined titles mapped to Senior Consultant; Junior Consultant mapped to Associate Consultant. Title and years_exp are the authority.
- The 6+ yrs rule was applied from years parsed out of the JD text (lower bound >= 6 dropped); years_exp is parsed by regex and may pick up a preferred rather than a required figure. A few kept roles show 5-10 or 3-8 ranges (A&M Business Technology, TDI) and are left in.
- Summary, qualifications and keywords were extracted heuristically from the JD text; jd_text is the full portal text.
- Not covered / blockers: Grant Thornton and BDO non-US/UK member firms, Protiviti outside US, Arup posting dates, Slalom ids below 3500, Infosys Consulting own portal (Akamai), Carbon Trust and South Pole dates.
