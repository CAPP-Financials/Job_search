# Tier 3A coverage (run 2026-10-15, window posted_date >= 2026-10-08)

Totals: 108 roles kept across 8 of 15 firms (Sia 66, Wavestone 16, PA 10, Kearney 7, Oliver Wyman 4, OC&C 3, Roland Berger 1, Dalberg 1). Output: data/raw/tier_3A.jsonl (validated: schema keys, band and track enums, dates >= 2026-10-08, 3-line summaries, non-empty required quals, jd_text >= 500 chars, no replacement chars, no duplicate URLs, no ask with min 6+ years).
Discovery used only each firm's own portal or the ATS behind it (public JSON/Atom/RSS APIs, plain HTML, one Playwright render for Simon-Kucher and Kearney). No LinkedIn or job boards. Firecrawl used once (L.E.K. JD page, returned a human-verification page). Nothing was applied to, signed into, or submitted.

Common exclusion rules: Manager/Principal/Director/Partner+, interns and campus/new-grad intakes (judged from the JD: dropped when the only experience asked is internships or a first job, or it is a graduate cohort), minimum 6+ years (min 5 kept), back-office roles, and technical roles with no honest A or B fit (cybersecurity/OT security, SAP configuration, data/ML/software engineers and architects, IT audit, legal, M365 support). Same rules as the 2026-10-10 sweep.

## Data quality notes
- posted_date_source: portal for Sia, Wavestone, PA, Roland Berger, Oliver Wyman, Dalberg; relative_text (estimated) for Kearney; none for OC&C.
- Years of experience are regex-extracted from JD text and may be null where the JD states none.
- sponsorship_flag: "likely-needed" is the default for non-India roles; "stated-not-offered" where the JD asks for existing work authorisation or clearance (OW US, PA UK clearance, Dalberg Geneva); "n/a-local" for OW Hyderabad.
- Salaries captured where posted: OW Boston $135,000-$155,000 and OW NY $190,000-$240,000.
- Track tags: only 2 non-A roles: Sia Consultant Energy, Utilities & Low Carbon Solutions (AB) and Dalberg Consultant (B). Everything else is A.
- Several Sia French/Dutch/Belgian roles are written in French or Dutch-English and are 0-3 yr Consultant grade.
- India coverage: the only India role in the window is OW Hyderabad Analyst/Senior Analyst (Private Capital).
