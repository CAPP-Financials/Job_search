## Cross-cutting notes
- posted_date_source values: Accenture relative_text; Arup and Carbon Trust none; everything else portal (Capgemini = index date, Slalom = import-stamped date).
- sponsorship_flag set from JD text only; roles in India tagged n/a-local; null where nothing stated. Many Guidehouse/Protiviti/FTI US roles show stated-not-offered (citizenship, clearance or work authorisation language).
- Band mapping: "Senior Associate" at A&M/Mercer/BDO mapped to Associate or Senior Analyst; Carbon Trust "Senior Associate" mapped to Associate; IBM "Sr Management Consultant" mapped to Senior Consultant (may be managing-level, check). Title and years_exp are the authority.
- Several kept roles list years_exp above the 2-5 band (Arup 6+/8+, ICF excluded, ZS 5-8, Capgemini OCM Senior 5+, IBM Sr Business Analyst 8+); left in, sort on years_exp.
- Not covered / blockers: Infosys Consulting own portal (Akamai), Grant Thornton and BDO non-India/non-US/UK member firms, Protiviti outside US, Arup posting dates, BDO UK 140 of 349 unseen.
