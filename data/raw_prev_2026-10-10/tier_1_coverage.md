# Tier 1 coverage (McKinsey, BCG, Bain) - swept 2026-10-10, window posted_date >= 2026-10-03

- **McKinsey & Company**
  - Method: portal's own JSON gateway, `GET https://gateway.mckinsey.com/apigw-x0cceuow60/v1/api/jobs/search?pageSize=100&start=<page 1..7>&lang=en` (host and gateway id read from the page's Next.js runtimeConfig; endpoint path from the JobSearch JS chunk). Plain `requests` works for the gateway; www.mckinsey.com itself times out / HTTP2-resets from this IP for requests and playwright (bot-block), Firecrawl rendered the listing but only 20 of 634 jobs per "Load More".
  - Postings seen: 634 (numFound 634, all 7 pages fetched). Kept: 39.
  - Date caveat: the portal exposes NO posted date. The API only has `postedToLinkedInDate` (504 of 634 populated, some future-dated 2027). Used it as posted_date for the 2 in-band roles inside the window (113072 on 2026-10-09, 102501 on 2026-10-05) with source "portal"; treat as a proxy. 37 kept roles have no date at all (posted_date null, source "none"): evergreen Business Analyst / Associate / Junior Associate / Fellow / Consultant requisitions. They are included because the rule says undated roles are kept; many are multi-office requisitions (e.g. 15136 Business Analyst lists 109 offices incl. India).
  - Excluded after JD read: 112907 Senior Expert Digital Marketing (India, 15+ yrs), manager/AP/EM/principal roles, QuantumBlack engineers and data scientists, interns, finance/HR/legal/admin. Dated in-window roles that were non-consulting or out of band were dropped (e.g. 102502 Delivery Manager, 112916/112915 software engineers).
  - Back-end ATS evidence: apply URLs `https://mckinsey.avature.net/careers/ApplicationMethods?folderId=<id>` (jobApplyURL field in API); `jobs.mckinsey.com/careers/Login?` page loads CSS from `templates-static-assets.avacdn.net` and `/portal/44/...` paths. Back-end = Avature. Front-end = Sitecore + Next.js (`_next/static`, `/api/jobs/search`).

  - Postings seen: 675 (all fetched, 675 unique). Dates: `t_create` epoch from the API (portal date). Kept: 2 (60106 Senior Analyst Corporate Finance & Strategy BCG ValueScience, San Francisco, 2026-10-09; 59977 AI Tech Consultant Bogota & Santiago, BCG Platinion, 2026-10-06).
  - Near misses, not kept: 59981 BI&A Senior Analyst Gurgaon (internal finance reporting, 6-8 yrs finance); 59960 Global People Analytics Senior Analyst Gurgaon (JD is software engineering for internal tooling); 59761 Consultant Turkiye (posted 2026-10-01, outside window); 59751 Senior Analyst BCG Vantage Gurgaon (2026-10-01, outside window); 59678 Chrome River Techno-Functional Consultant (2026-09-28). In-window rest of portal is Manager+/director/engineer/intern/HR/admin. No sustainability-track (B) role in the window.
  - Back-end ATS evidence: Eightfold. `careers.bcg.com/global/en/job/<id>` is a Phenom front-end (listing markers "phenom"), Apply leads to `experiencedtalent.bcg.com/careerhub/explore/jobs/<id>?...utm_source=Phenom` (Eightfold); canonicalPositionUrl also appears on `studenttalent.bcg.com/careers/job/<id>`. Req ids: ats_job_id 6xxxx = `display_job_id` 2026-6xxxx.
